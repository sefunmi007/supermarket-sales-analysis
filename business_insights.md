# Supermarket Sales Analysis — Business Insights

## 1. Overall Performance

The supermarket generated total revenue of **₦322,966.75** from **1,000 transactions**, with **5,510 units sold**. Gross income was **₦15,379.37**, while the average transaction value was **₦322.97**.

## 2. Branch Performance

**Branch C** generated the highest revenue at **₦110,568.71**, accounting for **34.2%** of total revenue. It also recorded the highest average transaction value at **₦337.10**.

Branch C generated the highest revenue despite recording the lowest transaction count among the three branches. This shows that transaction volume does not always correspond directly with revenue.

## 3. Product Line Performance

**Food and Beverages** generated the highest revenue at **₦56,144.84** and the highest gross income at **₦2,673.56**. It also recorded the highest average customer rating at **7.11**, compared with the overall average rating of **6.97**.

**Electronic Accessories** recorded the highest quantity sold at **971 units**, while **Fashion Accessories** recorded the highest number of transactions at **178**.

## 4. Customer Performance

Customers recorded as **Member** generated **₦164,223.44** in revenue compared with **₦158,743.31** from customers recorded as Normal.

Members recorded a higher average transaction value of **₦327.79**, compared with **₦318.12** for Normal customers.

Female customers generated **₦167,882.93** compared with **₦155,083.82** from male customers. Their average transaction value was **₦335.10**, compared with **₦310.79** for male customers.

## 5. Payment Behaviour

**E-wallet** recorded the highest transaction count at **345**, followed by Cash at **344**, while Credit Card recorded **311 transactions**.

Despite having one fewer transaction than E-wallet, **Cash generated the highest revenue at ₦112,206.57**.

## 6. Monthly Performance

**January** generated the highest revenue at **₦116,291.87** from 352 transactions.

Revenue declined to **₦97,219.37** in February before increasing to **₦109,455.51** in March.

February represents the weakest revenue month within the quarter and may warrant further investigation into the factors affecting sales performance.

# Business Recommendations

1. **Review Branch C's performance drivers.** Investigate the factors contributing to its higher average transaction value and revenue.
2. **Maintain focus on Food and Beverages.** Monitor inventory availability and customer demand for this strong-performing product line.
3. **Investigate the February revenue decline.** Review product availability, promotions, customer traffic, and operational factors.
4. **Monitor payment-channel performance.** Track both transaction volume and transaction value across payment methods.
5. **Review Health and Beauty performance.** Investigate demand, pricing, availability, and customer feedback.
